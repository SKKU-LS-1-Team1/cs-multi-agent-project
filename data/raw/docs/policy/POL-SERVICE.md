# Customer Service Levels and Escalation

## 1. Priority definitions

| Priority | Definition |
|---|---|
| P1 | Critical — safety hazard (smoke, fire, electric shock, arc) or complete site/line shutdown |
| P2 | High — production or operation affected, commissioning blocked, repeated trips |
| P3 | Normal — technical issue with equipment still operable; standard warranty, return or order cases |
| P4 | Low — information request (documents, specifications, quotation, certificates) without urgent deadline |

## 2. First response targets (hours)

| Priority | PLATINUM | GOLD | STANDARD |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | 1 | 2 | 4 |
| P2 | 4 | 8 | 12 |
| P3 | 8 | 16 | 24 |
| P4 | 24 | 48 | 72 |

## 3. Resolution targets (hours, before category factor)

| Priority | PLATINUM | GOLD | STANDARD |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | 24 | 48 | 72 |
| P2 | 72 | 120 | 168 |
| P3 | 120 | 168 | 240 |
| P4 | 168 | 240 | 336 |

Category factors: TECH_SPEC x1.0, INSTALL x1.0, WARRANTY x1.5, ORDER_LEAD x0.5, MANUAL x0.5, DEFECT x1.0, COMPLIANCE x1.0, QUOTE x1.0, FW_UPDATE x1.0, RETURN x2.0.

Time waiting for customer information is excluded from the service level clock.

## 4. Escalation

Cases are escalated to specialist teams (Field Service, Quality Assurance, Firmware R&D, Certification, Sales Operations) according to internal routing rules. Safety-related cases (P1) are handled by phone 24x7.
