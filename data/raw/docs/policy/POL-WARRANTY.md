## 2. Coverage

The warranty covers defects in material and workmanship: repair or replacement of the defective part or unit. On-site labour is covered for products installed by certified partners.

## 3. Exclusions

- Operation outside the specification (wrong supply voltage, overload, ambient conditions)
- Water ingress, mechanical damage, unauthorised modification
- Wear parts (fans, batteries, filters) after 12 months

## 4. Claim procedure

1. Report the failure with serial number, PO/invoice, installation date, description and photos.
2. The Warranty & RMA Desk evaluates the claim; Quality Assurance may analyse the cause.
3. If approved, an RMA number is issued and a replacement is shipped (standard lead time applies).
4. The decision is communicated within the service level defined in POL-SERVICE.
