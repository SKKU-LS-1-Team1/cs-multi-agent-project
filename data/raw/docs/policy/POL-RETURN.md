# Return and Exchange Policy

## 1. Return types

| Case | Condition | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Dead on arrival (DOA) | Reported within 14 days of delivery | Free replacement |
| Wrong item shipped | Our error, reported within 14 days | Free exchange; we arrange pickup |
| Shipping damage | Reported within 7 days with photos of packaging | Replacement; claim handled with carrier |
| Ordering error / excess stock | Unused, original packaging, within 30 days | Credit note minus 15% restocking fee |
| Used or installed units | — | Not accepted (warranty claim instead) |
| After 30 days | — | Not accepted |

## 2. Procedure

1. Request an RMA with PO/invoice number, serial numbers, reason and photos.
2. Quality-related returns (DOA, recurring defects) are reviewed by Quality Assurance.
3. Non-defective returns are handled by Order & Logistics.
4. Ship to the address on the RMA form. Units without RMA are refused.
