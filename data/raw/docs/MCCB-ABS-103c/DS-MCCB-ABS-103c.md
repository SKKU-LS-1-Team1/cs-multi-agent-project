# MCCB 100AF (MCCB-ABS-103c) — Datasheet

## 1. Overview

Molded case circuit breaker, 100A frame, thermal-magnetic trip unit. Product family: Low-voltage circuit breakers. Lifecycle status: **ACTIVE**.

## 2. Technical specifications

### 2.1 Ratings

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Frame size | 100AF |
| Rated current (In) | 15, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 75, 100 A |
| Rated operational voltage (Ue) | AC 690 V max |
| Number of poles | 2P / 3P / 4P |

### 2.2 Performance and characteristics

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Ultimate breaking capacity Icu at AC 415 V | 37 kA |
| Service breaking capacity Ics | 75% of Icu |

### 2.3 Endurance and communication

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Mechanical endurance | 8,500 operations |
| Electrical endurance | 1,500 operations at In |

### 2.4 Protection and options

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Magnetic trip setting | Fixed, 10 x In |

## 3. Installation data

### 3.1 Terminals

- Terminal tightening torque: 3.5 N·m (M5)
- Dimensions (W x H x D): 75 x 130 x 60 mm
- Weight: 0.9 kg
- Degree of protection: IP20 (front IP40)
- Operating temperature: -5 to +40 °C (derating above 40 °C)
