# Low-voltage circuit breakers — Frequently Asked Questions

## Q1. Which products does this FAQ cover?

MCCB 100AF (MCCB-ABS-103c), MCCB 400AF (MCCB-ABS-403c), ACB 2000A (ACB-AS-2000), ELCB 200AF (ELCB-EBN-203c)

## Q2. What is the warranty period?

MCCB-ABS-103c: 18 months; MCCB-ABS-403c: 18 months; ACB-AS-2000: 24 months; ELCB-EBN-203c: 18 months. See POL-WARRANTY.

## Q3. What is the standard lead time?

MCCB-ABS-103c: 4 weeks ex-works; MCCB-ABS-403c: 5 weeks ex-works; ACB-AS-2000: 8 weeks ex-works; ELCB-EBN-203c: 4 weeks ex-works. See POL-ORDER.

## Q4. Which certifications are available?

MCCB-ABS-103c: CE, CB, KC; MCCB-ABS-403c: CE, CB, KC, UL (-U variant); ACB-AS-2000: CE, KC, CB; ELCB-EBN-203c: CE, KC

## Q5. Where can I get manuals and datasheets?

Each product has a datasheet (DS-), installation guide (IG-), troubleshooting guide (TG-) and release notes (RN-) or maintenance guide (MG-). Documents are in English.

## Q6. What information should I send when reporting a fault?

Product model / ordering code, Serial number (nameplate), Error code or symptom displayed, Operating condition at failure (load, speed, temperature), Photos of product and nameplate, Load current / temperature records.

## Q7. How do I return a product?

Request an RMA number first. Returns without RMA are refused. See POL-RETURN.

## Q8. What is the latest firmware?

ACB-AS-2000: 1.4
