# Variable frequency drives — Frequently Asked Questions

## Q1. Which products does this FAQ cover?

LSLV S100 7.5kW VFD (INV-LSLV0075-S100), LSLV H100 22kW VFD (INV-LSLV0220-H100), iS7 75kW VFD (INV-LSLV0750-iS7)

## Q2. What is the warranty period?

INV-LSLV0075-S100: 24 months; INV-LSLV0220-H100: 24 months; INV-LSLV0750-iS7: 24 months. See POL-WARRANTY.

## Q3. What is the standard lead time?

INV-LSLV0075-S100: 4 weeks ex-works; INV-LSLV0220-H100: 5 weeks ex-works; INV-LSLV0750-iS7: 6 weeks ex-works. See POL-ORDER.

## Q4. Which certifications are available?

INV-LSLV0075-S100: CE, UL, KC; INV-LSLV0220-H100: CE, UL, KC; INV-LSLV0750-iS7: CE, UL, KC

## Q5. Where can I get manuals and datasheets?

Each product has a datasheet (DS-), installation guide (IG-), troubleshooting guide (TG-) and release notes (RN-) or maintenance guide (MG-). Documents are in English.

## Q6. What information should I send when reporting a fault?

Product model / ordering code, Serial number (nameplate), Error code or symptom displayed, Operating condition at failure (load, speed, temperature), Photos of product and nameplate, Event / trip log export, Parameter backup file, Motor nameplate data.

## Q7. How do I return a product?

Request an RMA number first. Returns without RMA are refused. See POL-RETURN.

## Q8. What is the latest firmware?

INV-LSLV0075-S100: 3.10; INV-LSLV0220-H100: 2.40; INV-LSLV0750-iS7: 4.10
