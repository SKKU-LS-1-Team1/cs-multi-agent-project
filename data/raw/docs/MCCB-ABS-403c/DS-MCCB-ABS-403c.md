# MCCB 400AF (MCCB-ABS-403c) — Datasheet

## 1. Overview

Molded case circuit breaker, 400A frame, adjustable thermal-magnetic trip unit. Product family: Low-voltage circuit breakers. Lifecycle status: **ACTIVE**.

## 2. Technical specifications

### 2.1 Ratings

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Frame size | 400AF |
| Rated current (In) | 250, 300, 350, 400 A |

### 2.2 Performance and characteristics

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Ultimate breaking capacity Icu at AC 415 V | 50 kA |
| Service breaking capacity Ics | 100% of Icu |
| UL 489 interrupting rating at 480 V (-U variant) | 35 kA |

### 2.3 Endurance and communication

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Mechanical endurance | 7,000 operations |

### 2.4 Protection and options

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Thermal trip adjustment | 0.8 to 1.0 x In |
| Magnetic trip adjustment | 5 to 10 x In |

## 3. Installation data

### 3.1 Terminals

- Terminal tightening torque: 18 N·m (M10)
- Dimensions (W x H x D): 140 x 257 x 109 mm
- Weight: 5.2 kg
- Degree of protection: IP20 (front IP40)
- Operating temperature: -5 to +40 °C (derating above 40 °C)
