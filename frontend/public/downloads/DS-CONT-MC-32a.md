# Magnetic Contactor 32A (CONT-MC-32a) — Datasheet

## 1. Overview

Magnetic contactor 32A (AC-3), 3-pole, 1a1b auxiliary contacts. Product family: Motor control devices. Lifecycle status: **ACTIVE**.

## 2. Technical specifications

### 2.1 Ratings

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Rated operational current AC-3 at 400 V | 32 A |
| Motor power AC-3 at 400 V | 15 kW |
| Auxiliary contacts | 1a1b built-in |

### 2.2 Performance and characteristics

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Coil voltage options | AC 24/48/110/220/380/440 V, DC 24/48/110/220 V |
| Coil pick-up voltage | 80-110% of rated coil voltage |

### 2.3 Endurance and communication

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Mechanical endurance | 10 million operations |
| Electrical endurance AC-3 | 1.2 million operations |
