# Cast Resin Dry-Type TRF 630kVA (TRF-DRY-630) — Installation and Commissioning Guide

## 1. Safety

Installation, wiring and commissioning must be performed by qualified personnel. Disconnect and lock out all power sources before work. Verify absence of voltage.

## 2. Receiving and storage

Inspect the packaging on arrival and report damage within 7 days (see POL-RETURN). Store indoors in a dry place between -25 and +55 °C, relative humidity below 85% without condensation.

## 3. Mounting and clearances

- Required clearances: Minimum 500 mm from enclosure walls; maintain electrical clearances per IEC 61936-1
- Operating temperature: -25 to +40 °C
- Degree of protection: IP00 (IP21/IP23 enclosure option)
- Above 1,000 m altitude, apply derating per the datasheet.

## 4. Wiring and tightening torques

- Terminal tightening torque: see dimension drawing
- Use copper conductors rated for at least 75 °C.
- Separate power and signal cables by at least 200 mm.

## 5. Commissioning procedure

1. Measure insulation resistance and polarisation index
2. Check off-circuit tap position against required secondary voltage
3. Verify temperature monitoring alarm and trip contacts
4. Check earthing of core and enclosure
5. Energise at no load and record secondary voltages

## 6. Pre-energisation checklist

- [ ] Product rating matches the drawing
- [ ] All terminals tightened to specified torque
- [ ] Protective earth connected
- [ ] Insulation resistance measured and recorded
- [ ] Settings / parameters recorded

## 7. Common commissioning issues

| Symptom | Likely cause | See |
|---|---|---|
| Winding temperature alarm (140 °C) | Cooling fans not operating under high load | TG-TRF-DRY-630 §3.1 |
| Abnormal humming noise | Loose core clamping or harmonic load | TG-TRF-DRY-630 §3.2 |
| Low insulation resistance before energisation | Moisture absorption during storage | TG-TRF-DRY-630 §3.3 |
| Secondary voltage too high | Off-circuit tap at wrong position | TG-TRF-DRY-630 §3.4 |
