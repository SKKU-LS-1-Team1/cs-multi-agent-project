# ACB 2000A (ACB-AS-2000) — Datasheet

## 1. Overview

Air circuit breaker 2000A, draw-out type, electronic trip unit (LSIG). Product family: Low-voltage circuit breakers. Lifecycle status: **ACTIVE**.

## 2. Technical specifications

### 2.1 Ratings

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Rated current (In) | 2000 A |

### 2.2 Performance and characteristics

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Ultimate breaking capacity Icu at AC 415 V | 85 kA |
| Short-time withstand current Icw (1 s) | 65 kA |

### 2.3 Endurance and communication

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Closing time | ≤ 70 ms |
| Mechanical endurance (with maintenance) | 20,000 operations |

### 2.4 Protection and options

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Trip unit protection functions | L (long-time), S (short-time), I (instantaneous), G (ground fault) |

### 2.5 Monitoring and auxiliaries

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Motor operator control voltage | AC/DC 110-240 V |
| Trip unit communication | Modbus RTU (RS-485) |
