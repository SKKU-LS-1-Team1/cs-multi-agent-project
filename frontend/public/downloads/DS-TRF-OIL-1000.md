# Oil-immersed Transformer 1000kVA (TRF-OIL-1000) — Datasheet

## 1. Overview

Oil-immersed transformer 1000kVA, 11/0.433kV, ONAN, conservator type. Product family: Power transformers. Lifecycle status: **ACTIVE**.

## 2. Technical specifications

### 2.1 Ratings

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Rated power | 1000 kVA (ONAN) |
| Voltage ratio | 11 kV / 0.433 kV |
| Vector group | Dyn11 |

### 2.2 Performance and characteristics

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Impedance voltage | 5% at 75 °C |
| Off-circuit tap range | ±2 x 2.5% |
| Insulating liquid | Mineral oil (IEC 60296) |

### 2.3 Endurance and communication

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| No-load loss | 1,100 W |
| Load loss at 75 °C | 9,500 W |

### 2.5 Monitoring and auxiliaries

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Top-oil temperature alarm / trip | 90 °C / 100 °C |
| Protection devices | Buchholz relay, pressure relief device, oil & winding temperature indicators |
