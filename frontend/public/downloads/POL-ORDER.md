## 2. Order confirmation

Order confirmations with an ex-works date are sent within 2 business days of PO receipt.

## 3. Expediting and partial shipment

Expedite requests are reviewed with production planning and may involve an expediting fee. Partial shipment from stock is possible on request.

## 4. Incoterms

Standard terms: FOB Busan or EXW Factory. CIF/CFR/DAP to the destination port are quoted on request.

## 5. Quotations

Quotations are valid for 30 days. Volume discounts apply to larger quantities. In regions with an authorised distributor, end-user requests are forwarded to the distributor.
