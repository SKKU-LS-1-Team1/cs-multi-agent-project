# MV Switchgear Panel 12kV (SWGR-MV-12kV) — Datasheet

## 1. Overview

12kV air-insulated metal-clad switchgear, withdrawable VCB. Product family: MV breakers & switchgear. Lifecycle status: **ACTIVE**.

## 2. Technical specifications

### 2.1 Ratings

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Rated voltage | 12 kV |
| Rated busbar current | 1250 A (up to 2500 A) |

### 2.2 Performance and characteristics

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Rated short-time withstand | 25 kA / 3 s |
| Internal arc classification | IAC AFLR 25 kA 1 s |
| Loss of service continuity | LSC2B-PM |
| Power frequency withstand | 28 kV / 1 min |
| Lightning impulse withstand | 75 kV |
